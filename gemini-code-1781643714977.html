Give a Little Bit

91 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by JustinGuitar

The Golden Rule: Focus entirely on the economy of motion. The gap between the A7 and D chords is tiny, so hover your fingers close to the strings to ensure a clean, crisp transition.

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

Fast acoustic rock feel:

Down Down Up Up Down Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: The Strike

Form the A7 shape. Strum heavily Down - Down.

Step 2: Quick Shift

Quick shift to D. Strum Up - Up - Down - Up to finish out the measure.

Step 3: Repetition

Repeat the A7 to D sequence twice.

Step 4: Shift to G

Shift to G. Strum Down - Down - Up - Up - Down - Up.

Step 5: The Walk

Walk it up to A. Strum Down - Down - Up - Up - Down - Up. Repeat the whole cycle!

Practice Notes

  • Load the track up in Chordify to lock in the exact timing of the chord changes.
  • Use your custom web-based metronome app to build up the tempo on the upbeat rhythm.

Gear Note

🎸 Play on a clean channel on the Spark Go to let the open strings ring out clearly.

About a Girl

121 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by Marty Music

The Golden Rule: For the chorus, keep your index, ring, and pinky locked in the power chord shape as you slide up and down the neck. Do not lift your fingers completely off the strings!

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

Driving eighth notes:

Down Down Down Up Down Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: The Verse Groove

Start on the open Em chord. Hit the lower strings heavily with two Down strums.

Step 2: Transition to G

Catch the upstroke as you land the G chord: Down-Up, Down-Up. Let all strings ring.

Step 3: The Chorus Climb

Slide your power chord grip to the 4th fret A string for C#5. Strum Down-Down.

Step 4: Drop to G#5

Drop to the thick E string, 4th fret for G#5. Strum Down-Down.

Step 5: Slide to F#5

Slide down two frets to F#5. Strum Down-Down-Up-Down-Up.

Practice Notes

  • The verse uses open chords with a loose, slightly syncopated feel. Keep the wrist relaxed.
  • Keep the strumming hand moving constantly like a pendulum to maintain that gritty groove.

Gear Note

🎸 Kick on a bit of crunch on the Spark Go amp for the chorus sections to get that authentic 90s drive.

Listen to Her Heart

124 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by Lauren Bateman

The Golden Rule: The F#m Barre requires leverage. Squeeze with your thumb firmly behind the neck, and ensure your index finger is perfectly straight applying even pressure.

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

Classic Rock 4/4:

Down Down Up Up Down Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: Anchor the A

Anchor on the open A major chord. Strum Down - Down. Let the jangle happen.

Step 2: The Barre Drop

Brace your index finger for the barre and drop into F#m. Strum Up - Up - Down - Up. Make sure the high strings aren't deadened.

Step 3: Release to D

Release the barre and jump to open D. Strum Down - Down.

Step 4: Shift to E

Shift fingers to E major. Strum Up - Up - Down - Up.

Practice Notes

  • Pay close attention to the driving jangle-pop feel. The rhythm needs to stay consistently tight.
  • If the barre chords get tiring, practice those capo-based shortcuts using G and Am shapes to keep the session going.

Gear Note

🎸 Sounds great dialed in with a classic rock tone. Switch to the bridge or middle pickup for bite.

Overdose

128 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by The Stellar Guitarist

The Golden Rule: Fret Hand Muting. This song relies on silence as much as sound. Mute the unused strings with your index finger so only the intended power chord notes ring out.

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

Heavy, staccato downstrokes:

Down Down Down

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: The A5 Hit

Start on the open A5 power chord. Hit it hard with a single Downstroke and immediately mute the strings with your right hand palm.

Step 2: Jump to C5

Jump to the C5 power chord on the 3rd fret A string. Downstroke and mute.

Step 3: Slide to D5

Slide up two frets to D5. Downstroke and mute.

Step 4: Drop to G5

Drop to the thick E string 3rd fret for G5. Downstroke, mute, and jump immediately back to the open A5 to restart the riff.

Practice Notes

  • This is a fantastic workout for locking in those power chords. Keep your grip firm but relaxed enough to slide quickly.
  • The rhythm is very deliberate. Hit the chords hard and cut them off cleanly to match the groove.

Gear Note

🎸 Dial up the gain on your amplifier and switch over to the bridge pickup for maximum bite.

Helpless

58 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by Lauren Bateman

The Golden Rule: Let it Breathe. This is a beautifully simple progression. Focus on a very relaxed, swaying rhythm. Don't rush the tempo; let the chords ring out naturally.

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

Slow, sweeping 4/4 time:

Down Down Up Down Down Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: The Sweeping D

Start on the open D chord. Give it a slow, sweeping Down strum, followed by a lighter Down-Up to fill out the measure.

Step 2: Transition to A

Transition to the open A chord. Keep the same relaxed rhythm: Down, Down-Up.

Step 3: Linger on G

Switch to G. Because the G lasts twice as long in the progression, play the full pattern: Down, Down-Up, Down, Down-Up.

Step 4: Repeat the Loop

Repeat indefinitely! The entire song sits on this beautiful D-A-G loop.

Practice Notes

  • A great endurance exercise for clean transitions.
  • Try throwing in some gentle arpeggiations (picking individual notes within the chord) on the G chord to add flavor.

Gear Note

🎸 Keep the tone warm and clean. Select the neck pickup for a fuller, rounder sound.

Tennessee Whiskey

49 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by Lauren Bateman

The Golden Rule: The 6/8 Waltz Swing. The song has a very distinct, soulful 6/8 swing. Think in groups of three (1-2-3, 4-5-6) and accentuate the first beat of each measure to lock into that R&B groove.

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

Slow 6/8 waltz feel:

Down Up Down Up Down Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: Anchor the A

Anchor on the open A chord. Think in counts of 3. Strum Down-Up-Down-Up-Down-Up for two full measures. Accentuate the first beat heavily.

Step 2: Prepare for the Jump

Prepare for the big jump. Lock in your index finger across the 2nd fret and clamp down the Bm shape.

Step 3: Hold the Bm

Hold the Bm for the same duration. Down-Up-Down-Up-Down-Up for two measures.

Step 4: Release and Repeat

Release the barre and fall back to the open A. Take your time, letting the 6/8 swing guide the transition.

Practice Notes

  • This is the ultimate song to master that Bm chord we've been tackling. Squeeze tight and make sure the A string is ringing clearly.
  • Load it up in Chordify to really get a feel for how long each chord lingers before the change.

Gear Note

🎸 Add a touch of reverb on your Spark Go and use the middle pickup position for a balanced, throaty R&B tone.

Heart of Gold

85 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by JustinGuitar

The Golden Rule: Anchoring Fingers. Watch the quick transitions in the chorus. The move from Em to C to D to G happens relatively fast. Practice anchoring your fingers where possible to make the changes smoother.

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

Classic driving acoustic:

Down Down Up Down Up Down Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: The Iconic Intro

Start on Em. Strum a solid Downstroke. Let it ring for a beat.

Step 2: Quick Fill

Follow with a quick Down-Up, Down-Up on the Em.

Step 3: Shift to D

Shift immediately to D. Strum a single, sharp Downstroke.

Step 4: Resolve the Phrase

Quickly shift to Em for one final sharp Downstroke to resolve the phrase.

Step 5: The Chorus Run

For the Chorus, follow the rapid sequence: Em (Down-Down) to C (Up-Down-Up) to D (Down-Down) to G (Up-Down-Up).

Practice Notes

  • Keep the strumming hand bouncing naturally. The acoustic drive of this song relies on a very consistent right-hand motion.

Gear Note

🎸 Roll off the tone knob just a bit to mimic a warm acoustic resonance while playing clean.

Wish You Were Here

62 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by The Stellar Guitarist

The Golden Rule: Amber stresses that before you pick a single note, you must "glue" your third (ring) finger and fourth (pinky) finger down on the 3rd fret of the B and high E strings. Keep them completely anchored throughout this entire sequence!

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Fill Pattern

When transitioning between licks, fill the chord space with this rhythm phrase:

Down Down Up Up Down Down Up Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Part 1: Primary Loop (Repeat Twice)

Step 1: The Entry Lick

Pick fret 3 on Low E (finger 2). Pluck open A, immediately hammer-on to fret 2 (finger 1). Hit open D.

e|-----------------------|
B|----3------------------|
G|----0------------------|
D|----0-----------0------|
A|---------0_2-----------|
E|----3------------------|
Step 2: Hit the Em7 Fill

Drop finger 1 onto fret 2 of the D string. Strum the 8-beat pattern (D D U U D D U U), starting downward from the D string.

Step 3: The Resolving Walk

With your fingers remaining in the shape, execute this single-note line across the D and G strings:

D|----2----0----2----0----|
Step 4: Hit the G Major Fill

Form a full G Major layout (leaving the bottom two strings anchored) and execute the full 8-beat pattern.

Part 2: Routing into the A7sus4 Change

Step 5: Rerun & Em7 Drop

Rerun the exact structural lick from Step 1, then execute the full strum across the Em7 layout.

Step 6: Descending Transition Lick

Walk your pick backward from the D string up to the A string using this run:

D|----2----0--------------|
A|--------------2----0----|
Step 7: Drop into A7sus4

Press down across the 2nd frets of the D and G strings. Strike the chord using your core 8-beat framework.

Part 3: The Climax Outro

Step 8: Extended A7sus4 Strumming Run

Repeat the entry loop back to the A7sus4. To build dynamic tension like Amber, stretch the strum layout to this sequence:

D • D • U • U • D • D • U • D • U • D • D • U • U
Step 9: Resolution

Shift squarely back to a standard G Major chord with an open rolling cadence to move cleanly into the main vocal tracking lines.

Practice Notes

  • Load the track up in Chordify to lock in the exact timing of the chord changes when the main vocal verses begin.

Gear Note

🎸 Dial in a very clean, warm acoustic-style setting. On the Strat, flip to the neck pickup for maximum warmth to emulate that acoustic resonance.

Country Feedback

74 BPM

Intro Breakdown • As Taught by John McNicholas

The Golden Rule: Not only is this your first verse, it's every verse. The progression repeats over and over until the very end, making it an incredibly easy song to lock into.

Required Chord Shapes

The Rhythm Pattern

A steady acoustic strumming pattern with quick passing hits:

Down Down Up Down Up

Strum-by-Strum Breakdown

Step 1: Em to G

Form the Em chord and strum Down twice (Down - Down). On the final beat before changing, hit a quick Up strum on your way to G. Land on G and strum Down twice (Down - Down).

Step 2: D to C

Transition to D with a quick Up strum, then strum Down twice on the D (Down - Down). Hit another quick Up strum on the transition, then land on C. The C chord lasts twice as long, so strum it out fully: Down - Down - Up - Down - Up.

Step 3: The Turnaround (G to D)

Restart the momentum by shifting back to G and strumming Down twice (Down - Down). Catch the transition with an Up strum, then form the D chord and strum Down twice (Down - Down).

Step 4: Resolve on Cmaj7

Hit a quick Up strum as you transition, then land on Cmaj7 (standard C shape with the index finger lifted). Let this dreamy chord ring out for the full measure by strumming Down - Down - Up - Down - Up. Repeat the entire sequence!

Practice Notes

  • The shift from C to Cmaj7 in the second half of the phrase simply requires lifting your index finger. Take advantage of this simple, beautiful change!

Gear Note

🎸 Keep the acoustic tone bright. Let the open strings on that Cmaj7 ring out fully to capture the R.E.M. vibe.
